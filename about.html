About us

3+ years of bringing kicks back to life

We have been in the sneaker cleaning industry for over three years, and Cape Town's sneakerheads keep sending us their pairs.

MKICKZ started with a simple idea: a clean pair changes how you feel when you walk out the door.

Over the years we have cleaned everything from kids' school sneakers to collector pairs like Jordans, Air Force 1s and Nike runners. Every material needs different care, so we treat suede, nubuck, canvas, mesh and leather each in their own way.

We also got so particular about what goes on your shoes that we made our own sneaker cleaning solution, proudly South African and biodegradable. It is the same formula we trust on our clients' pairs, now available for you to use at home.
